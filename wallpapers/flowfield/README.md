# Flow Fields

Thousands of thin curves traced through a smooth field of directions. The look is meant to suggest long-exposure light, silk, or streams of ink.

Some fields swirl into eddies, which suits the silk variants, and others bend the curves so they gather into veins. On dark backgrounds the lines glow, on paper backgrounds they soak in like ink, and on the pastel background they sit on top as light.
