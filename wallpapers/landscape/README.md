# Layered Landscapes

Simple layered landscapes: mountain and hill silhouettes fading into haze under a gradient sky with a sun or moon.

Distant ridges fade toward the colour of the sky near the horizon, so they look pale and catch the light, and mist collects in the valleys. Hillsides split into lit and shadowed sides along soft diagonal lines, which is what gives the dunes and the alpine rock faces their shape.

Depending on the variant, a scene can also have a glowing sun, a full or crescent moon, stars, streaky or puffy clouds, pointed or round trees, snow caps, cliffed headlands, and water that reflects the scene with ripples and a sparkling path of light.
