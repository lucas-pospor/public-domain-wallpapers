# Aurora / soft gradient

Calm, glowing colour fields loosely based on the northern lights. Each image has a smooth sky gradient made of soft blobs of colour, with one or two aurora curtains on top. A curtain has a gently curving bright lower edge, a long fade upward, and soft vertical rays. The dark variants also have a few faint stars. Fine film grain and a little dither keep the gradients from showing visible bands.
