# Geometric

Low-poly, mosaic and tiling wallpapers.

The low-poly images are made of triangles from a slightly irregular grid, each shaded as a small tilted surface under a single light. The mosaics are irregular cells of similar size separated by smooth dark lines, and each pane has its own slight tilt, a glow in the middle and a glass texture. Two regular tilings round out the set: a hexagon grid where each hexagon is lit like a shallow six-sided pyramid, and a grid of equilateral triangles coloured by a gradient.
