# Cosmic

Deep-space scenes where everything is invented. Nothing is based on or traced from a real astronomical image.

The nebulae are built from layered, warped noise that forms bright knots, faint wisps and thin filaments, with each colour in its own region. Dark dust lanes block the gas and the stars behind them. Each scene has tens of thousands of stars, mostly faint, with colours that follow real star temperatures. The bright ones have halos, and a few large stars have a wide glow that lights up the gas around them. Some variants include a banded gas giant with a soft line between day and night and a glowing rim of atmosphere, and in one the planet has rings that cast and catch shadows.
